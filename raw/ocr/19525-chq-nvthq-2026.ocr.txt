The RRU is composed of a transceiver board, an
amplifier and a filter.

- Transceiver board : under the control of the DU,
it only converts analogue signals into digital
signals and digital signals into analogue signals,

and converts signals between radio frequency
(RF) and baseband;

- Amplifier : it expands the effective range of the
antenna by amplifying RF signals;

- Filter : it removes noise to provide the RF
characteristics required for the DU and the
antenna.

For the cellular wireless communication function
of a base station, the RRU receives scheduling
and synchronisation signals from the DU, and
then converts them into RF signals according to
the relevant RF band through RF filtering and
amplification. The RRU _ itself cannot
independently transmit or receive signals without
control from the DU within a network and is not
compatible with other communication apparatus.
